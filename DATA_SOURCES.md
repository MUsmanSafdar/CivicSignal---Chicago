# Data sources and attribution

Licence notes are summaries. Confirm current terms at each source before redistributing data, and keep the attribution below.

| Dataset | Used for | Source and attribution | Licence / terms note |
|---|---|---|---|
| FEMA National Risk Index (NRI) | Hazard risk scores by county | Federal Emergency Management Agency, National Risk Index. hazards.fema.gov/nri | Published by FEMA as public data. Check the NRI data-resources page for the current usage terms. The sample files in this package use invented values shaped like NRI fields. |
| CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index (SVI) | Vulnerability scores by county | Centers for Disease Control and Prevention / Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry, SVI. atsdr.cdc.gov/place-health/php/svi | U.S. government work, public domain; CDC/ATSDR asks that SVI be cited. Only the single snapshot loaded in this package is shown; no multi-year trend is displayed or implied. |
| OpenFEMA | Disaster declarations, assistance and mitigation project history (as a source for loss history) | FEMA OpenFEMA. fema.gov/about/openfema | Free and open under OpenFEMA terms and conditions; FEMA does not endorse derived products. Cite OpenFEMA and the dataset name. |
| National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) claims and policies | Past-loss history, community participation | FEMA via OpenFEMA; Community Status Book for participation status | Public, with redaction of personal data in the published sets. Verify participation status in FEMA's Community Status Book before relying on it. |
| Grants.gov | Funding Watch notices | Grants.gov public Search2 API (api.grants.gov). No key required | Public federal data. Notices change; open the notice on Grants.gov for status and deadlines. |
| Illinois cooling-center map layers | Cooling center list | Public Illinois and Chicago-area cooling-center layers; list refresh date is shown in the app | Hours change often. Always call 311 or 211. |
| Project, sponsor and capacity files in `data/` | Demonstration | Invented sample data created for this prototype | No licence needed; not real records. |

## Citation text
"Hazard and vulnerability figures: FEMA National Risk Index; CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index. Funding notices: Grants.gov. Loss history: OpenFEMA and NFIP. Sample values are illustrative unless the page states that verified data is loaded."
