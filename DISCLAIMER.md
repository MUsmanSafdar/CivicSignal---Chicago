# Disclaimer

**Eligibility output is administrative screening. It is not a legal determination and not legal advice.**

- The eligibility engine compares the facts on file with the wording of the cited regulations (for example 44 CFR 77.6 for Flood Mitigation Assistance and 44 CFR 206.434 for the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program). A reviewer must approve each rule's wording before it counts.
- The engine is deliberately cautious: a missing or unrecognized fact gives "Needs review", never a pass. "Needs review" is a hold, not a denial.
- Only FEMA, the State, or the program's official administrator can make an eligibility determination. Program rules, notices of funding opportunity and policy change; check the current ones.
- Scores and rankings depend on weights a person chooses and on data that may be sample, incomplete or out of date. They show how a judgment call moves the list; they do not decide anything.
- Benefit-cost ratios must come from FEMA's approved Benefit-Cost Analysis methodology. The tool records the ratio you enter and does not calculate one.
- Nothing here is an emergency alert or an official forecast. In an emergency call 911.
- Decision packets record what was shown to an official and what that person decided. They do not replace the official file.

If you need a legal opinion, consult your agency's counsel.
